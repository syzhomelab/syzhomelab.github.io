01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I work where
engineering meets
defence.

My career started in infrastructure and technical support, including work with WA Police. I then moved into blue-team security operations at Water Corporation, investigating alerts and supporting network, endpoint and firewall controls.

Today, my focus is on engineering the platforms behind security operations. At Protect+ / Kinetic IT, I've worked across customer SIEM environments — connecting log sources, troubleshooting data quality, building KQL detections, managing analytics rules through Azure DevOps and integrating endpoint and identity security. I also maintain vulnerability-management services with Qualys and Tenable, support identity controls through Entra ID and Active Directory, and deploy CrowdStrike Identity Protection.

That mix of hands-on engineering and operational experience is what I bring to every environment: I understand how a platform should be built, but also what analysts need when an alert fires.
